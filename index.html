Commander Swipe

Swipe through card ideas for your Commander deck

Pasting an updated version of this deck (picks added, cards cut) keeps your swipes and cuts. Working on another deck? Start a different deck; this one's swipes stay saved.
  1. Paste your deckYour Moxfield Export, as text.
  2. SwipeRight to add, left to skip, up for maybe.
  3. Copy backPaste your picks into Moxfield.

On your deck in Moxfield: More → Export → Copy for Moxfield. Then come back, paste it into the box (or press Paste from clipboard), and press Load deck.
