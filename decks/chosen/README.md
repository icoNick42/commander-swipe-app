# Your chosen opponents

Opponent decks you chose to mix into the sim tables. They never rotate out and skip the power window. About one table in `chosen_one_in` (in `decks/pools.json`, 3 to start) gets one of them, in a seat a rotation or gauntlet deck would have taken. Add as many as you like.

- **Add one:** on the sims page's Decks tab, paste the Moxfield export and choose "A chosen opponent". Or put the Moxfield export here as `<name>.txt` (on github.com: open the `sims` branch, this folder, then Add file). Put the commander first or under a "Commander" heading. The board turns it into `<name>.dck` and scores its power.
- **Drop one:** remove it on the Decks tab, or delete its `.txt` and `.dck` here.
- **Picks are different:** a pick (`decks/picks/`) sits at every table.
