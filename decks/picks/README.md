# Your picks

Opponent decks you chose. They never rotate out, and one sits at every sim table when any are here.

- **Add one:** paste a Moxfield link or export into a Claude chat and say "add to my picks", or put the Moxfield export here as `<name>.txt` (on github.com: open the `sims` branch, this folder, then Add file). Put the commander first or under a "Commander" heading. The board turns it into `<name>.dck` and scores its power.
- **Drop one:** delete its `.txt` and `.dck`.
